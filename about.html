Hi!

My name is Alan, and I am an aspiring animation filmmaker from Kazakhstan. I am passionate about directing and animation, with a strong interest in visual storytelling and exploring the artistic possibilities of cinema.

Through my work, I aim to create original stories that combine expressive visuals, meaningful ideas, and unique artistic styles. I enjoy experimenting with animation techniques, character design, composition, lighting, and color to develop distinctive visual worlds and bring my ideas to life. I am particularly interested in independent filmmaking and 2D animation, where I can combine my artistic vision with cinematic storytelling.

My goal is to continue developing my skills as a director and animator and create films that inspire audiences, evoke emotions, and offer new perspectives on the world.
