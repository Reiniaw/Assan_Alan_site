Faceless

Synopsis

A young man who frequently writes poetry, inspired by his favorite dark writer, lives a double life, trying to imitate the writer he admires. However, his true personality eventually takes over.

Film Stills

Film Excerpts

The film explores the idea that the world doesn't need another dark writer — it needs you to be yourself. You can look up to your idols for inspiration, but by adopting their image, habits, and mannerisms, you risk losing your own identity.
