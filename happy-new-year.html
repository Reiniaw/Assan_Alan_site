Happy New Year!

Synopsis

On New Year's Eve, Santa awakens on the outskirts of a city and discovers that its residents seem to have forgotten all about the holiday. He notices a towering building with a giant loudspeaker on its roof and realizes that he must play a record containing a song that could bring back the New Year's spirit and restore even a small spark of happiness to people's hearts.

However, there's one problem: the tallest building is inhabited by all kinds of monsters, making his journey far more difficult than he ever imagined.

Character

I experimented with different designs and realized that Santa needed a completely new look, as the classic image of a chubby old man with a sack of presents didn't fit the story.

Film Excerpt

I spent a lot of time experimenting with lighting and visual presentation, paying particular attention to shot composition and color grading.
