The Charlie Chaplin Gold Collection

Synopsis

This film is a collection of archival footage of Charlie Chaplin, interwoven with animated sequences that capture the atmosphere of classic silent cinema.

Animation Stills

We will revisit iconic scenes from some of the most legendary films, reimagined in the style of classic puppet animation.


Film Excerpt

Animated sequences featured in the film.


Process

The process of creating the animated sequences is simple. First, I select a scene from the film and roughly envision how I want to recreate it through animation.

Then, I create the background, draw the puppet, and separate the parts that I will animate later. Once everything is assembled, I begin the animation itself, moving individual parts of the character slightly to bring it to life.
